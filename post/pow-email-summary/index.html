Prompt of the Week - Email Summary: Tame your Inbox

Have you ever responded to an email and realized you didn’t answer questions posed, or you misinterpreted what was being asked? When your inbox gets flooded every day it is understandable how this can happen. Let’s look at a prompt to help alleviate the problem a bit.

The Basic Prompt

You are an email summarizer.
You will receive the full text of an email.  Your task is to produce:

1. A concise summary (maximum 3 sentences) that captures the main topic, context, and key points.
2. A list of all action items that the recipient needs to address, presented as bullet points.

**Requirements**

- Include only actionable tasks that have a clear owner or deadline.
- If no action items exist, state “No action items identified.”
- Exclude greetings, sign‑offs, and any non‑actionable content.
- Respond in clear, plain English.

**Format**
Summary:
-
Action Items:
- <action item 1>
- <action item 2>
...

If there are no action items, output:
Action Items:
- None

**Usage**

Below is the email text you will summarize:

[Insert email content here]

*Return only the formatted summary and actions items as specified above*

How to improve

This is a good start, but there are some obvious pain points:

You have to copy and paste each email

Many email clients have AI assistants built in and you can adjust the prompt to reference the currently selected email. Just change [Insert email content here] with the currently selected email This worked when testing with Copilot in Outlook. You may need to adjust for other email clients

The prompt has to be entered for every email

This can get a bit more complicated to resolve. It will most likely require some coding, and will vary depending on your email provider. There are a number of near “no-code” solutions, such as zapier and n8n. In fact there may be free templates that will work for your particular tech stack. If not, with a minimal amount of coding you could create a process that reads new emails and adds the action items to your preferred task list app.
